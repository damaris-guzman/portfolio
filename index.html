Damaris Guzman

Damaris Guzman is an interdisciplinary new media artist whose practice transforms screen light into embodied experience. Working across animation, net art, digital fabrication, and projection mapping, she examines how perception, technological embodiment, and posthuman identity mutate as code infiltrates flesh, asking who truly holds agency. ♥( ˆ⌣ ˆԅ)

Damaris Guzman earned a BFA in Film and Digital Media, with a focus in Film Studies, from the University of California, Santa Cruz. She is currently an MFA candidate and Teaching Associate in Digital Media Art at San José State University. ♡☆♡☆
